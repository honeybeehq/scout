Say what's wrong.
Point at it.

Use your app and talk. Scout turns what you say and what you point at into ready-to-run tasks for your coding agents, transcribed on your Mac.

Download for Mac

macOS 26 or later · Open source, MIT

  1. 1
    Recording bar. Press ⌃⌥S in any app. The bar hangs from the top of the screen and shows the words it hears as you talk.
  2. 2
    Review window. After you stop, every task is laid out on a timeline. Trim, split, join, edit, then send.
  3. 3
    Menu bar panel. Start scouting, see recent tasks and where they went, reopen earlier sessions.

From a sentence to a task.

Each task says what you said, shows what you saw and names the element you pointed at. With the web SDK, it names the source file too.

Review, then send

Each task is an outlined region on a filmstrip of the session. Drag an edge to trim; edges snap to your pauses. Speech that isn't in a task yet stays visible, so nothing you said gets lost.

  • ⌘B Split
  • ⌘J Join next
  • ⌘Z Undo
  • ⌘↩ Send task

Two ways to record

⌃⌥S Walkthrough
One bundle with the transcript and word timings, key frames, what you pointed at and drew, and a short list of asks. Its path goes on the clipboard, ready for ⌘V into Apiary. ⌃⌥B starts a new ask.
Record as tasks
Pick it in the menu bar. Scout cuts the stream into tasks while you talk, on pauses, cue words like "next" and changes of app or target. Or set New task to ⌃⌥B and cut only when you say so.

Five minutes, six tasks

One session can hold many pieces of feedback, across apps. Scout follows the pane or window under your pointer, so the clip is about what you were looking at.

Point, don't describe

Hover and Scout notes what you rest on. Hold ⌥ and click to pin an element. Hold ⌥ and drag to draw.

The bar shows what it is capturing. It follows your pointer; ⌃⌥E widens it.

Pick your transcriber

The built-in Apple transcriber is ready at once and writes your words live. Whisper large-v3-turbo writes the whole transcript again after you stop, after a one-time download of about 650 MB. Both run on your Mac.

Send it anywhere

Every task works as plain text, so any agent or chat can take it.

  • ClipboardPaste into any agent. Copy all copies every task at once.
    Always
  • hiveGoes to the session already working in the app's repo, or a new one.
    Optional
  • ApiaryTasks and attachments render the same as in hive.
    Optional

Stays on your Mac

  • Transcribed on your Mac. Speech never goes to a server to be written down.
  • Nothing leaves until you send it. You see each task, and can edit it or leave things out, first.
  • Discard means gone. ⌃⌥X twice deletes the recording and its files. Nothing is sent or copied.
  • No account needed. hive and Apiary are optional. Without them, tasks go to the clipboard.

Try it on your next build.

Setup checks each permission live and brings you back when it's granted. You can skip any of them and grant it later.
