From a sentence to a task.

Each task says what you said, shows what you saw and names the element you pointed at. With the web SDK, it names the source file too.

Review, then send

Each task is an outlined region on a filmstrip of the session. Drag an edge to trim; edges snap to your pauses. Speech that isn't in a task yet stays visible, so nothing you said gets lost.

  • ⌘B Split
  • ⌘J Join next
  • ⌘Z Undo
  • ⌘↩ Send task
The review window

Two ways to record

⌃⌥S Walkthrough
One bundle with the transcript and word timings, key frames, what you pointed at and drew, and a short list of asks. Its path goes on the clipboard, ready for ⌘V into Apiary. ⌃⌥B starts a new ask.
Record as tasks
Pick it in the menu bar. Scout cuts the stream into tasks while you talk, on pauses, cue words like "next" and changes of app or target. Or set New task to ⌃⌥B and cut only when you say so.

Sending tasks

Pick your transcriber

The built-in Apple transcriber is ready at once and writes your words live. Whisper large-v3-turbo writes the whole transcript again after you stop, after a one-time download of about 650 MB. Both run on your Mac.

Choosing a transcriber

Stays on your Mac

  • Transcribed on your Mac. Both transcribers, Apple and Whisper, run on the Mac itself.
  • Nothing leaves until you send it. In the review you can edit a task, or leave out what you pointed at, before it goes.
  • Discard throws it away. ⌃⌥X twice stops the recording and deletes what it wrote. Nothing is sent, and the clipboard stays as it was.
  • hive and Apiary are optional. Without them, each task ends on the clipboard, ready to paste.
