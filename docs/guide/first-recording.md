# Your first recording

1. Open the app you want to give feedback on.
2. Press `⌃⌥S`, or click *Start scouting* in the menu bar panel. Recording starts at once. There is no window or screen to pick.
3. Talk about what you see. Say what is wrong and what you want instead.
4. Point at things. Rest the pointer on an element, or hold `⌥` and click it to pin it.
5. Press `⌃⌥S` again, or click *Finish* in the recording bar.

By default the recording ends as a walkthrough, and Scout asks where to send it. See [Walkthroughs and tasks](modes.md).

## The recording bar

While you record, a bar hangs from the top centre of the display being captured. From left to right it shows the elapsed time, your voice level, the last words Scout heard, what is being captured, *Discard* and *Finish*. On a MacBook with a notch, the bar wraps around the notch so no control is hidden. The bar is not part of the recording and can't be dragged.

## What gets recorded

Scout follows the pointer. It records the pane under the pointer, or the window when it can't tell panes apart, or the screen when no window is under the pointer. An outline shows the captured area, and the rest of the screen is dimmed a little.

- When you keep moving between two areas, Scout suggests keeping both in view with `⌃⌥E`. It never widens on its own.
- `⌃⌥E` keeps both recent areas in view. Press it again to widen to the window, then the screen, then back to following.
- `⌃⌥⇧E` goes back to following the pointer.
- Clicking the capture button in the bar does the same: click to widen, `⇧`-click to follow.

## Pointing

- **Hover.** An element the pointer rests on for a moment is attached to what you are saying.
- **Aim and pin.** Hold `⌥` and an outline follows the element under the pointer, with a label naming it. Click to pin it. The click goes to Scout, not to the app. Release `⌥` to stop aiming.
- **Draw.** Hold `⌥` and drag to draw on the screen.

## Throw a recording away

Click *Discard* in the bar, or press `⌃⌥X`. Scout asks "Discard this recording?" and keeps recording while the question shows. Press `⌃⌥X` again or click *Discard* to confirm; press `Esc` or click *Keep* to carry on. Discarding deletes everything the recording wrote and sends nothing. Once a task from the recording has been sent, it can't be discarded.

## Voice commands

These phrases are removed from the task text:

- "new task" or "next task": start a new task.
- "scratch that": drop the current task.
- "send it": close the current task and send it now.

They apply when you record as tasks. In a walkthrough, saying "new task" doesn't start a new ask; use `⌃⌥B`.
