# Updates

Scout updates itself with Sparkle.

- **Automatic checks** are on by default. Scout checks once a day and asks before it installs anything. Turn this off in Settings → General → Updates → *Automatically check for updates*.
- **Check now** with *Check for Updates…* in the menu bar panel or in Settings → General → Updates. Settings also shows the version you have.
- **While you record**, Scout doesn't interrupt you. *Check for Updates…* is available when the recording ends, and an update found during a recording is shown after it ends.
- **When an update is ready**, the menu bar icon gets a small dot, and the panel shows "Update available" or "Restart to update" in place of *Check for Updates…*.

Updates are signed, and Scout only installs updates signed with the project's key.

You can always download the latest version by hand from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/honeybeehq/scout/releases/latest).
