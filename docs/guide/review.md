# The review window

The review window is where a finished recording made with *Record as tasks* becomes tasks you are happy to hand off. It opens after you finish when a task is waiting (you can change this in Settings → General), and *Review* in the menu bar panel opens the latest session. *Sessions* in the window lists earlier ones.

The window has three columns: the task list, the video with the selected task's pins, and the task's details with its destination and send buttons at the foot.

## Fix the splits

The timeline under the video shows the whole recording as a filmstrip, with each task outlined.

- Drag a task's edge to trim it or move the boundary between two tasks. Edges snap to pauses in your speech; hold `⌥` to place them freely.
- `⌘B` splits the selected task at the playhead, `⌘J` joins it with the next one, `⌘⌫` deletes it and `⌘Z` undoes.
- Sent tasks are locked.

Speech that isn't in any task shows as dashed amber on the timeline and under "Not in a task yet" in the list, with *New task* and *Add to task N*.

## Edit before sending

The title and the transcript are editable; *Use the original* brings back what Scout wrote. Elements under "Pointed at" can be left out with *Leave out*. Pins show the frame at the moment you pinned.

## Send

Each task has its own destination, *Copy task* and *Send task* (`⌘↩`). The header has *Send all to…*, which sets one destination for every waiting task, and *Send all* (`⌥⌘↩`). When every waiting task goes to the clipboard, the button reads *Copy all*. See [Sending tasks](sending.md).

## Discard a session

The trash button in the header asks "Discard session?" and then deletes the session's video, signals and review, and its waiting tasks. A session with a sent task can't be discarded.
